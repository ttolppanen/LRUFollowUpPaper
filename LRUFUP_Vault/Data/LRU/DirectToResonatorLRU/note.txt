You can connect a transmon directly to the resonator and get better detuning,
because the resonator does not have anharmonicity. If you connect 20 -> 02, you get
T* = 600ns I suppose, which is better than in our paper. The T1 is then 280mus.
On the other hand, you can connect 20 -> 11, and have even larger detuning. The optimal
removal rate is quite high, which then leads to fast leakage removal but also poor T1
for the qubit. But, if you do not operate with the optimal removal rate, but instead
something smaller, like 2x the optimal rate in the 20 -> 02 case, then the T1 is a lot
better than in the 20 -> 02 case because of the larger detuning, and the T* is 
comparable, if not faster.
